Pick And Mix Candy

Pick and mix is simple, just a wall of open bins full of sweets, tongs, a plastic bag with a funny cartoon character on it, and complete freedom. You get to choose what goes in, and you pay by weight at the end. There will never be two bags that are the same, which is what make it fun!

For me, it’s pure childhood! I remember standing in front of those bins and having the hardest dilemma of my life: Should I pick the gummy with sour sugar? Is one more candy really worth 1 more dollar? Every time it takes forever deciding, and the bag always feels heavier than it really was.

That’s why I built this site! To check out each bins, take a closer look at every sweet, and get a bit of that feeling back. Browse the bins below, spin each candy around, and find out more about them!
